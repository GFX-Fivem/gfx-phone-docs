# Free and Full Version

The free and the full version are **the same resource**. A server runs the full version while a paid
GFX Phone purchase is bound to it; otherwise it runs the free version. Upgrading never needs a new
download or a restart.

## What the free version includes

Phone (calls, video calls, contacts), Messages, Camera, Photos, Maps, Weather, Clock, Notes, Calendar,
Wallet (Lemon Pay), Music, Apps (App Store), Settings, Mail, Ments, Dark Chat, Adverts, Garage,
Services, Files, Voice Memos, Compass, Calculator and the games (Chess, Blockade, Sugar Rush, 2048,
Snake, Mines) - plus the Dynamic Island, widgets, the admin panel, 13 languages and
[custom apps](../developers/custom-apps/).

## What the full version adds

**Apps:** Swipe, Picsta, Fansv, Flame, Snapz, JobIn, Gather, News, Health, CarLink, GoThere, Renty,
Home, Crate, Eatsy, Binix, Critterra, ID Cards, Racing, Passwords, and Bills (Bills is free when your
server uses its own billing script).

**Features:** voicemail, call recording, stickers, live location and polls in Messages, sharing ID
cards, Pixel Pet, LemonBuds (wireless earbuds) and saving passwords in apps.

In the free version these apps stay on the phone and show a locked screen. The lock is enforced on
the server, not only in the interface.

Compare and buy: **[gfxscripts.com/phone#pricing](https://gfxscripts.com/phone#pricing)**

## Activating the full version

- **Web:** a server you register at [phone.gfxscripts.com](https://phone.gfxscripts.com) with a paid
  purchase is full right away. To activate later: **Overview > Phone licence**, enter the Tebex
  transaction id.
- **In game:** the setup wizard's first step, **Phone licence**, or **Overview > Phone licence** in
  `/phoneadmin` (super admin).

Bought it but the panel still says free? Press **Check now** (or **I already bought it**): the panel
asks for your purchase immediately instead of waiting for the next periodic check.

## Good to know

- One purchase activates one server. A purchase can move to another server once.
- If a purchase is refunded or a subscription ends, the server drops to the free version and keeps
  its panel and data.
- The licence is checked about every 30 minutes; a short outage of the licence service does not turn
  your phone off.
